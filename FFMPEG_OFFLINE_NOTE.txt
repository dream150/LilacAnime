Offline MP4 conversion
======================
New offline downloads use Media3 Transformer to convert the fully downloaded
local HLS playlist into episode.mp4.

FFmpegKit is intentionally not included. libmpv already ships its own native
FFmpeg libraries, and combining another FFmpegKit native stack caused an
ABI/SONAME collision (for example av_set_saf_open).

Legacy Media3 downloads remain supported and are migrated/read through the
existing offline bridge.
